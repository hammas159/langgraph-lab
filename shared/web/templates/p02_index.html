Does the router know when it's guessing?

A LangGraph supervisor node classifies a ticket and states its own confidence. Some tickets genuinely touch more than one category. The question is whether the router's confidence tracks whether it is actually right — and whether gating on it, or voting across differently worded prompts, catches the cases where it doesn't.
